Kartly

An online grocery store with a full admin panel. I designed the interface and built both the shop and the back office.

Year
2024
Timeline
1 month
Role
Design and development
Stack
PHP, MySQL, Bootstrap 5

Overview

Kartly lets customers browse groceries by category, search for products, add them to a cart and check out. Signed-in customers can also see their past orders.

Behind the shop is an admin panel for managing products, categories and orders, with a dashboard that shows how the store is doing.

The goal

A store that looks clean and stays easy to use on any screen size, without hiding the things people need most: categories, the cart and the status of their orders.

What I built

  • Search and category filters, so products are quick to find.
  • Cart, checkout and an order history for signed-in customers.
  • An admin dashboard with totals for users, products, orders and revenue.
  • Product, category and order management, including printable order slips.
  • Login with access control, so only admins can reach the back office.
