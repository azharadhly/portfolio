(Case)Automation · Onboarding

Typeform to Slack approvals

New proposals from a Typeform go straight to HR and the CEO in Slack. Once both approve, the person is onboarded automatically.

Year
2024
Timeline
2 weeks
Role
Automation design and build
Stack
Typeform, Slack, Make.com

Overview

When someone submits the proposal form, Make.com sends a private Slack message with the details to both HR and the CEO. After both have approved, the new member is added to the company’s welcome invite and to its public Slack channel.

The goal

Every request had to reach the right two people, nothing could move forward until both had said yes, and personal details had to stay in private messages.

What I built

  • A Typeform trigger that turns each submission into a Slack approval request.
  • Conditional logic that waits for approval from both HR and the CEO.
  • Automatic onboarding: a welcome invite and access to the public Slack channel.

Stack

  • Typeform

  • Make.com
  • Gemini API
